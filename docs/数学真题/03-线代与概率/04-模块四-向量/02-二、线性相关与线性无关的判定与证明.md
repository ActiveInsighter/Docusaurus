### (二)抽象型向量组的线性相关性

A. $\displaystyle \text{有一组不全为} 0 \text{的数} k_1,k_2,\cdots,k_s \text{,使} k_1\alpha_1+k_2\alpha_2+\cdots+k_s\alpha_s\ne 0$

D. $\displaystyle \text{若} 0\alpha_1+0\alpha_2+\cdots+0\alpha_m=0 \text{,则} \alpha_1,\alpha_2,\cdots,\alpha_m \text{线性无关}$
