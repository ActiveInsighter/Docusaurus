# 二、多元函数导数的计算

## 小题

### （一）数值型

1. **【1987-45-4 分】** 已知 $\displaystyle z=\arctan \dfrac{x+y}{x-y}$，求 $\displaystyle dz$。

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\displaystyle dz=\dfrac{-y\,dx+x\,dy}{x^{2}+y^{2}}.
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 令

$$
\displaystyle u=\dfrac{x+y}{x-y},\qquad z=\arctan u.
$$

先求中间变量的偏导数：

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x}
=\dfrac{(x-y)-(x+y)}{(x-y)^{2}}
=-\dfrac{2y}{(x-y)^{2}},
$$

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial u}{\partial y}
=\dfrac{(x-y)+(x+y)}{(x-y)^{2}}
=\dfrac{2x}{(x-y)^{2}}.
$$

又

$$
\displaystyle 1+u^{2}
=1+\left(\dfrac{x+y}{x-y}\right)^{2}
=\dfrac{2(x^{2}+y^{2})}{(x-y)^{2}}.
$$

因此

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial z}{\partial x}
=\dfrac{1}{1+u^{2}}\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x}
=-\dfrac{y}{x^{2}+y^{2}},
$$

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial z}{\partial y}
=\dfrac{1}{1+u^{2}}\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial y}
=\dfrac{x}{x^{2}+y^{2}}.
$$

故全微分为

$$
\boxed{\displaystyle dz
=\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial x}\,dx
+\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial y}\,dy
=\dfrac{-y\,dx+x\,dy}{x^{2}+y^{2}}}.
$$

本题关键是使用复合函数求导法则，并先将 $\displaystyle 1+u^{2}$ 通分化简。

</details>

2. **【1988-5-4 分】** 已知 $\displaystyle u=e^{\frac{x}{y}}$，求 $\displaystyle \frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial x\partial y}$。

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\boxed{\displaystyle
\frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial x\partial y}
=-\frac{x+y}{y^{3}}e^{\frac{x}{y}}
}\qquad (y\ne0).
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 先对 $\displaystyle x$ 求偏导：

$$
\displaystyle
\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}
=\frac{1}{y}e^{\frac{x}{y}}.
$$

再对 $\displaystyle y$ 求偏导。由乘积求导法则，

$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle
\frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial x\partial y}
&=\frac{\partial}{\partial y}
\left(\frac{1}{y}e^{\frac{x}{y}}\right)\\
&=-\frac{1}{y^{2}}e^{\frac{x}{y}}
+\frac{1}{y}e^{\frac{x}{y}}
\left(-\frac{x}{y^{2}}\right)\\
&=-\left(\frac{1}{y^{2}}+\frac{x}{y^{3}}\right)e^{\frac{x}{y}}\\
&=-\frac{x+y}{y^{3}}e^{\frac{x}{y}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

函数及其偏导数要求 $\displaystyle y\ne0$。

</details>

3. **【1989-5-5 分】** 已知 $\displaystyle z=a^{\sqrt{x^{2}-y^{2}}}$，其中 $\displaystyle a>0$，$\displaystyle a\ne1$，求 $\displaystyle dz$。

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\displaystyle dz
=\dfrac{a^{\sqrt{x^{2}-y^{2}}}\ln a}{\sqrt{x^{2}-y^{2}}}
(x\,dx-y\,dy).
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 令

$$
\displaystyle u=\sqrt{x^{2}-y^{2}},
\qquad z=a^{u}.
$$

则

$$
\displaystyle dz=a^{u}\ln a\,du.
$$

而

$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle du
&=\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{x^{2}-y^{2}}}\,d(x^{2}-y^{2})\\
&=\dfrac{x\,dx-y\,dy}{\sqrt{x^{2}-y^{2}}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

故

$$
\boxed{\displaystyle dz
=\dfrac{a^{\sqrt{x^{2}-y^{2}}}\ln a}{\sqrt{x^{2}-y^{2}}}
(x\,dx-y\,dy)}.
$$

在实数范围内使用上述微分公式时，应取 $\displaystyle x^{2}-y^{2}>0$；边界处需另行讨论可微性。

</details>

<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

<div className="exercise-label-row">
<strong>答案：</strong>
<div className="exercise-label-body">

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial^{2}f}{\partial x\partial y}
=\dfrac{x^{2}-y^{2}}{x^{2}+y^{2}}.
$$

</div>
</div>

**解析：** 先对 $\displaystyle x$ 求偏导。注意

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial}{\partial x}
\arctan\dfrac{y}{x}
=-\dfrac{y}{x^{2}+y^{2}},
$$

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial}{\partial x}
\arctan\dfrac{x}{y}

\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle f_{x}

\end{aligned}

$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle f_{xy}

&=\dfrac{x^{2}-y^{2}}{x^{2}+y^{2}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

所以

$$
\boxed{\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial^{2}f}{\partial x\partial y}
=\dfrac{x^{2}-y^{2}}{x^{2}+y^{2}}}.
$$

本题先求 $\displaystyle f_{x}$ 后会出现显著约简，比分别对原式连续使用商法则更简洁。

$$

其中区域 $D_{uv}$ 为图中阴影部分，则 $\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial F}{\partial u}=$（　）

<div className="choice-options">
<div className="choice-option-row">A. $\displaystyle vf(u^{2})$ &emsp;&emsp; B. $\displaystyle \dfrac{v}{u}f(u^{2})$ &emsp;&emsp; C. $\displaystyle vf(u)$ &emsp;&emsp; D. $\displaystyle \dfrac{v}{u}f(u)$</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

$$

\displaystyle a=-\dfrac34,
\qquad

$$

$$
\displaystyle 2a^2-2b^2+3a+3b=0,

\displaystyle 2v_{xx}-2v_{yy}=0,
$$

即

\displaystyle v_{xx}-v_{yy}=0.

求 $\displaystyle f(u)$。

</div>

2. **【2008-34-10 分】** 设 $\displaystyle z=z(x,y)$ 是由方程

$$
\displaystyle x^2+y^2-z=\varphi(x+y+z)
$$

所确定的函数，其中 $\displaystyle \varphi$ 具有二阶导数且 $\displaystyle \varphi'\ne-1$：

（Ⅰ）求 $\displaystyle dz$；

（Ⅱ）记

$$
\displaystyle u(x,y)=\dfrac{1}{x-y}\left(\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial x}-\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial y}\right),
$$

求 $\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x}$。

<details>
<summary>查看答案与解析</summary>

**答案：** （Ⅰ）

$$
\boxed{
\displaystyle dz=
\dfrac{2x-\varphi'(x+y+z)}{1+\varphi'(x+y+z)}\,dx
+
\dfrac{2y-\varphi'(x+y+z)}{1+\varphi'(x+y+z)}\,dy
}
$$

（Ⅱ）

$$
\boxed{
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x}
=-\dfrac{2(2x+1)\varphi''(x+y+z)}{\left[1+\varphi'(x+y+z)\right]^3}
}
$$

**解析：** 记

$$
\displaystyle s=x+y+z.
$$

原方程为

$$
\displaystyle x^2+y^2-z=\varphi(s).
$$

（Ⅰ）对方程两边求全微分，得

$$
\displaystyle 2x\,dx+2y\,dy-dz
=\varphi'(s)(dx+dy+dz).
$$

将含 $\displaystyle dz$ 的项移到一边：

$$
\displaystyle \left[1+\varphi'(s)\right]dz
=\left[2x-\varphi'(s)\right]dx
+\left[2y-\varphi'(s)\right]dy.
$$

由于 $\displaystyle \varphi'(s)\ne-1$，故

$$
\displaystyle dz=
\dfrac{2x-\varphi'(s)}{1+\varphi'(s)}\,dx
+
\dfrac{2y-\varphi'(s)}{1+\varphi'(s)}\,dy.
$$

即

$$
\displaystyle z_x=\dfrac{2x-\varphi'(s)}{1+\varphi'(s)},
\qquad
z_y=\dfrac{2y-\varphi'(s)}{1+\varphi'(s)}.
$$

（Ⅱ）先计算两偏导数之差：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle z_x-z_y
&=\dfrac{2x-\varphi'(s)}{1+\varphi'(s)}
-\dfrac{2y-\varphi'(s)}{1+\varphi'(s)}\\
&=\dfrac{2(x-y)}{1+\varphi'(s)}.
\end{aligned}
$$

因此

$$
\displaystyle u(x,y)
=\dfrac{1}{x-y}(z_x-z_y)
=\dfrac{2}{1+\varphi'(s)}.
$$

对 $\displaystyle x$ 求偏导：

$$
\displaystyle u_x
=-\dfrac{2\varphi''(s)}{\left[1+\varphi'(s)\right]^2}s_x.
$$

又因为

$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle s_x
&=1+z_x\\
&=1+\dfrac{2x-\varphi'(s)}{1+\varphi'(s)}\\
&=\dfrac{2x+1}{1+\varphi'(s)}.
\end{aligned}
$$

所以

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x}
=-\dfrac{2(2x+1)\varphi''(s)}{\left[1+\varphi'(s)\right]^3}.
$$

代回 $\displaystyle s=x+y+z$，得

$$
\displaystyle \dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x}
=-\dfrac{2(2x+1)\varphi''(x+y+z)}{\left[1+\varphi'(x+y+z)\right]^3}.
$$

</details>
